## Welcome to Robotics
### A few classroom suggestions

1. Work from bell to bell on any curricular topics, preferably Robotics.
3. If you have an important reason to use your cell phone. Stand up so I know you are proud of what you are doing while your doing it.
4. The course top grade between 86%-100% will be determined by the industry need for softskills (learning together - not cheating). If you can do an assignment, help 3 others. I would prefer 10 students collaboratively get 100% in the course than 3 students competitivily get 86%.
5. Put "my" in front of any of your variables and function names. That way you and I know to not google about them.
6. ChatGPT etc can explain code for you, to reduce your reading I don't comment my code very much.
7. Learn VIDEO-FLAC for coding comfort (variables,in-out,decisions,events,objects - functions,loops,arrays,classes).
8. My coding course link at https://github.com/hpssjellis/high-school-javascript-game-dev-assignment-images#long-version-of-video-flac .
9. To reduce hardware needs I will teach one or more assignment per class, but many students should work ahead to see if the assignments work this year (not deprecated).
10. If you finish all assignments you have more time for your final projects.
11. I split Robotics into these areas: coding, sensing, accuators, IoT and Machine Learning.
12. To pass the course you need to make a level 1 individual final  project that connects a sensor with an actuator of your own interest.
13. To get an "A" your level 2 individual final project should take multiple sensors and/or multiple actuators, and/or IoT, and/or Machine learning. All projects include coding.
14. If finished early you can do a level 3 group final project. We expect people with working machine learning projects to combine with people doing IoT projects and/or multiple sensing actuator projects. All participants in the group should be bringing a skill to the group. Yes softskills are a skill (teamwork, communication, problem-solving, adaptability, and time management).
